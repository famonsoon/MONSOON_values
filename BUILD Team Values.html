BUILD Team Values

BUILD

In the end, we stick with BUILD as the acronym for new values.

BUILD stands for:

  1. B – Business Oriented
  2. U – Useful
  3. I – Initiative
  4. L – Learning
  5. D – Deliverability

B

B – Business Oriented

Bring strong business orientation by understanding the client, their brand, their goals and what matters to their business.

U

U – Useful

Be useful by creating real impact through your role and the contribution you make beyond it.

I

I – Initiative

Show initiative through proactivity. Act, suggest, and move things forward without waiting to be prompted.

L

L – Learning

Keep learning, improving, and getting better through experience.

D

D – Deliverability

Show deliverability through ownership — take responsibility for your work, deadlines, and commitments.
